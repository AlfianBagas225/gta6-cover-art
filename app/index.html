Palm Sunset Cover Studio

Your photo, seven poster layouts, six colour grades — a shareable cover in under a minute.

1 · Photos

Your pictures stay on this machine — pick a thumbnail to fill the selected panel.

Click a thumbnail to put that photo in the selected panel. Photos stay on your machine — nothing is uploaded.

2 · Layout — panel arrangement

Seven ways to arrange the panels. The purple tile is where the title sits — Neon Grid slots it into the middle row. Press L to step through the layouts.

3 · Grade — colour mood

Six colour moods. VHS adds scanlines, a red/cyan split and tracking bands over the whole cover. Press G to step through the grades.

4 · Selected panel — zoom & position

Frame the photo in the panel you clicked. Drag it on the cover, or use the sliders below.

Panel 1 of 4

Or drag the panel right on the cover, and scroll over it to zoom. Shift-click a second panel to swap the two photos.

5 · Caption — title & subtitle

Short titles read best at 300 px wide — the title opens large and the subtitle carries an outline.
